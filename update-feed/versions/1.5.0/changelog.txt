2b2m 1.5.0

- Adds Advanced Peripherals, Draconic Evolution, Evolved Mekanism, Ars Elemental, Apothic Compats, and new Create and Mekanism integrations.
- Updates the server runtime to NeoForge 21.1.248 and refreshes the supported mod set.
- Limits Advanced Peripherals player detection to 1024 blocks, with exact positions through 256 blocks and increasing position error beyond that range.
- Disables cross-dimensional player detection and additional player information.
- Removes MineColonies and its dedicated dependency stack.
